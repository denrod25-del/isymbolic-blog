Yesterday's story was Google's org chart. Today's is the courtroom. A New Mexico judge nearly tripled Meta's child-safety penalty and attached operational mandates to it, OpenAI filed its answer to Apple's trade secrets suit by attacking Apple's own security hygiene, and Suno — facing lawsuits on two continents — said it will start watermarking output. Meanwhile OpenAI quietly removed the last hard limit on free ChatGPT.

## Policy & Legal

**[New Mexico orders Meta to pay another $567 million](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/new-mexico-court-orders-meta-to-pay-additional-567m-in-child-safety-case/)**
That brings the state's total against Meta to $942 million, on top of a $375 million penalty in March. The more interesting part is the injunctive relief: within New Mexico, Meta must hide visible Like counts for under-18 users absent parental approval, pause push notifications to minors between 10 p.m. and 7 a.m., and cap underage usage at 90 hours a month — about three hours a day. The judge's theory was public nuisance. Meta says it will appeal. Fines are survivable; a court dictating product mechanics is a different kind of precedent.

**[OpenAI's defense against Apple: your own security was the leak](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/openai-says-apples-own-security-practices-undermine-its-trade-secrets-case/)**
OpenAI moved to dismiss, arguing Apple can't claim trade secret protection for information it didn't protect — Apple let employees use personal iCloud accounts for work and failed to revoke access after departures. The filing points to an Apple manager who stayed logged into former engineer Chang Liu's personal iCloud after he left, to transfer files. Apple had requested expedited discovery on August 4 and says more ex-employees may be involved. "You didn't guard it" is a real defense, not a rhetorical one.

**[Suno will watermark its songs](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/amid-legal-battles-suno-says-it-will-start-watermarking-songs/)**
The company is partnering with Musixmatch to use its Sentinel copyright-detection system, plus audio fingerprinting, and hinted at a new download policy without specifics or a date. Context: UMG and Sony suits coordinated by the RIAA, a late-July German ruling for GEMA, and a Massachusetts class action over the November 2025 breach that exposed training data scraped from YouTube, Deezer, and Genius. Suno raised $400 million in June. Watermarking after the training is the cheapest concession available.

## Model & Product

**[ChatGPT text chats are now unlimited on the free tier](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/openai-brings-unlimited-chatgpt-text-chats-to-free-users/)**
Free and Go users move to GPT-5.6 Luna, Plus and Pro get GPT-5.6 Sol for quick tasks, and both gain reasoning controls — a Think button on free, a thinking-depth slider on paid. OpenAI's internal numbers claim factual errors are 62% less common on Luna and 68% less common on Sol versus GPT-5.5-Instant. Those are vendor-reported. Files, images, voice, and image generation stay capped. With ChatGPT past a billion weekly users, giving away unlimited text inference is a statement about how cheap serving has gotten.

**[OpenAI's smart speaker will reportedly run $300–$400](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/openais-new-ai-smart-speaker-will-reportedly-sell-for-between-300-and-400/)**
Bloomberg reports a donut-shaped metal device with moving parts, designed with Jony Ive's LoveFrom, shipping in 2027. Amazon's speakers run $40–$240, so this is priced as a premium object rather than a loss-leader endpoint. Unnamed sources, no confirmed specs — treat the price as a target, not a rate card.

## Business

**[Naïve raised $28.5M to let agents run companies](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/naive-raises-28-5m-to-automate-the-grunt-work-of-setting-up-and-running-a-company/)**
Nexus Venture Partners led, with Y Combinator, Zetta, Liquid 2, and angels including Gokul Rajaram and former HubSpot COO JD Sherman; ~$32M raised total. The product bundles incorporation, payments, email, phone numbers, cloud, and storage behind one API, so a prompt in Cursor or Claude Code can spin up an LLC with a Stripe account. 30,000+ developer customers within months, run-rate revenue up 10x in six months to low double-digit millions, on ten employees.
